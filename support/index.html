Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email ronconapps@gmail.com.

To help us find the problem, please include:

  • your phone model and its Android or iOS version,
  • the version of the game (shown in the store listing),
  • what happened and what you expected,
  • for an online problem: the room code, if you have it, and roughly when it happened.

To remove the data the game keeps on your device, uninstall the game. For questions about data on our servers, see the Privacy Policy or email us.
